Buying property in Saudi Arabia — sak, Wafi off-plan, RETT (شراء عقار, صك, وافي, ضريبة التصرفات)
Also searched as: شراء بيت, شراء شقة, إفراغ, بيع على الخارطة, وافي, سكني, صك إلكتروني, off-plan, title deed.

1. Check the title (sak / صك)
Inquiry on owned properties: Real Estate Market / Najiz with Nafath; choose personal or PoA capacity; Real Estate Inquiries → Owned Properties; open the deed. https://my.gov.sa/en/services/19733
Paper or missing deeds can be digitized; electronic deeds can be amended without visiting a notary. https://my.gov.sa/en/services/99971
A buyer should confirm the electronic deed and that the seller can convey, using Najiz search. Power of attorney for a sale is issued on Najiz (electronic PoA max one year). https://my.gov.sa/en/services/19916

2. Off-plan (Wafi — بيع على الخارطة)
REGA: Wafi grants licenses for selling off-plan units and registers developers. Law basis published by REGA: Council of Ministers Resolution 196 dated 04/03/1445 AH. Call 199011 or use the Wafi platform.
Before paying: inquire licensed projects and qualified companies on https://rega.gov.sa/en/rega-services/real-estate-inquiries/
REGA off-plan sales licence lets a developer sell units under construction and receive instalments into an escrow account dedicated to the project at a bank licensed in the Kingdom. Licence prerequisites published by REGA include a valid building permit, ownership deed, consulting-office agreement, CPA agreement, sales-agreement form, and escrow-account agreement.
Nitaq licence is the developer qualification to practise off-plan sales. Do not pay cash outside the licensed escrow flow that REGA describes.

3. Real Estate Transaction Tax
ZATCA: 5% on real estate transactions. Current law in effect 10 April 2025 (12 Shawwal 1446 AH). Seller pays before or during conveyance. Sales are generally RETT not 15% VAT. First-home support for citizens is on ZATCA pages — read the live cap. https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/RETTRegulation.aspx

4. After agreement
Conveyance (ifragh) is a Ministry of Justice process via Najiz / notarial real-estate services. Search Najiz rather than inventing a button name. Pay RETT as ZATCA requires. Register any subsequent lease on Ejar if the unit will be rented.

5. Sakani
Sakani is the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing citizen housing programme. If a live sakani.sa page is not available in this compilation, tell the user to confirm the current portal URL from official housing / my.gov.sa search. Do not invent subsidy amounts.

Frequently asked questions
Q: How do I know the sak is real?
A: Najiz / Real Estate Market owned-properties or deed inquiry after Nafath login. https://my.gov.sa/en/services/19733
Q: Can I buy off-plan?
A: Only check REGA Wafi licensed projects and pay through the project escrow. https://rega.gov.sa/en/rega-services/platforms/wafi-off-plan-sales-and-lease/
Q: What tax?
A: RETT 5% unless exempt. https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/RETTRegulation.aspx
Q: Hello, I want to buy a house.
A: Ask whether ready unit (sak on Najiz) or off-plan (Wafi). Then RETT on zatca.gov.sa.
