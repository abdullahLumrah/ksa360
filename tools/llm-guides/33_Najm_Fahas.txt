Najm and Fahas — accidents and vehicle inspection
Najm (accident reporting and insurance coordination): https://www.najm.sa
Periodic vehicle inspection (Fahas / MVPI): use Absher and the authorised inspection centres listed for your city. Traffic and vehicle services also sit under Absher: https://www.absher.sa

Najm
Najm coordinates many traffic accident reports and insurance claim starts between the parties and insurers. After a collision, follow police / Najm instructions for your case type. Mild accidents may be handled through Najm processes when both sides are eligible. Do not invent a fine or a claim deadline. Najm and the insurer state the live steps.

Fahas (periodic inspection)
Vehicles need periodic technical inspection (commonly called Fahas) before istimara renewal can complete. Book and complete inspection at an authorised centre, fix any failed items, then continue istimara renewal in Absher. Inspection prices can vary by centre and vehicle type. Confirm at the centre; do not invent the fee.

Istimara and insurance
Istimara is the vehicle registration. Valid insurance is required. Absher shows vehicle status, violations, and many renewal steps for the owner.

Frequently asked questions
Q: Accident — police or Najm?
A: Follow the on-scene rule for your case. Najm handles many eligible minor accidents and insurance coordination. Serious injuries need police and emergency services first.
Q: Can I renew istimara without Fahas?
A: Periodic inspection must be valid when Absher requires it for renewal. Complete Fahas first if it is expired.
Q: Hello, my car failed Fahas.
A: Fix the failed items at a workshop, re-inspect, then continue Absher istimara renewal.
