MISA — foreign investment licences
Official platform: Ministry of Investment (MISA) https://misa.gov.sa
Related: Saudi Business Center https://business.sa

What MISA is
MISA licenses foreign investment activities in Saudi Arabia. A foreign investor who wants to own or operate a business may need a MISA investment registration or licence in addition to a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce. Exact activity eligibility and capital rules change. Confirm on misa.gov.sa.

How this differs from Premium Residency
Premium Residency (pr.gov.sa) is a residency product. MISA is an investment/business licensing track. An investor product under Premium Residency does not replace every MISA or Commerce requirement. Check both when someone asks about investing and living in Saudi Arabia.

Frequently asked questions
Q: Do I need MISA to work on an Iqama?
A: Ordinary employees sponsored by a Saudi company use Qiwa / Iqama. MISA is for investment and owning a foreign-invested business.
Q: Hello, I want to invest.
A: Open misa.gov.sa and business.sa. Confirm the activity is allowed and what licence is required.
