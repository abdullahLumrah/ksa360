Who can board
A resident (Iqama holder) leaving the Kingdom and planning to return needs a valid passport, a valid Iqama, and an Exit Re-Entry visa issued before travel. A resident leaving for good needs a Final Exit visa, not an Exit Re-Entry visa. Issue these on Absher or Muqeem. The employer often issues them for a worker. Dependents are linked to the sponsor's request.
A citizen boards an international flight with a Saudi passport. A domestic flight inside Saudi Arabia is normally boarded with a national ID or an Iqama. Confirm the airline's ID rule before you go to the airport.
A visitor uses the visa they entered on, and must leave before that visa expires. Overstay is a Jawazat matter, not an airline matter.

Exit Re-Entry and Final Exit — issue BEFORE the airport
National Platform (Absher service): log in to Absher, Visa Services, choose the person, enter the required information, issue Exit and Re-Entry (or Final Exit), then you can print the visa. https://my.gov.sa/en/services/269423
Do not go to the airport hoping to get the visa at passport control. The permit must already be in the system.

Absher Business user guide (establishment issuing visas) lists these conditions for Exit/Re-Entry:
- Sponsor, worker or family member must not be recorded as dead, runaway, or having an Istiqdam violation
- Visa fees paid; all traffic violations of the worker paid
- No unused-visa cancellation violation; no valid visa already issued
- The person must be inside the Kingdom when the visa is issued
- Passport validity 90 days or more
- Fingerprint exists (Absher Business guide: males aged 18 and older for Exit/Re-Entry)
- Primary visa holder must travel with all family members added to the passport
Source: Absher Business user guide — Issuance of exit/re-entry visa https://www.absher.sa

Absher Business user guide for Final Exit adds:
- Fingerprint for males and females aged 16 and older
- The person must not have a vehicle registered in the system
- Visa must be used within two months of issuance or fines apply according to the regulations
- Employer must destroy the worker's Iqama and is fully responsible for that
Source: Absher Business user guide — Issuance of final exit visa https://www.absher.sa

National Platform Muqeem Final Exit page lists similar conditions but states passport validity of 60 days or more, and fingerprint for males and females from 15 years and older. https://www.my.gov.sa (service “Issue Final Exit Visa - Muqeem”). When Absher Business text and the Muqeem National Platform page differ, follow the live Absher or Muqeem screen for that request. Do not invent a different number.

Cancellation (Absher Business user guide)
- Cancel Final Exit: within 60 days of issuance; person must be inside the Kingdom; unused-visa rules apply.
- Cancel Exit/Re-Entry: within 90 days of issuance or before the return date on the visa, whichever is first; person must be inside the Kingdom.
Fines for not cancelling unused visas follow the regulations. Do not invent a fine amount.

Extend Exit Re-Entry while the person is outside the Kingdom: Absher service “Extend Exit Re-Entry Visa for sponsorees outside kingdom” https://my.gov.sa/en/services/269426 — Iqama must be valid; pay the fee shown. One National Platform listing for extension through Absher shows a service cost of 200.0 on that page; always pay the amount Absher displays.

GACA passenger obligations (official, last updated 19/01/2026 on gaca.gov.sa/en/passenger-rights)
- Review the carriage-contract terms before booking
- Give the airline correct contact information
- Arrive at the airport at the time specified by the air carrier and be at the departure gate as instructed
- Complete travel documents for the country of departure, arrival, and any stopover
- Disclose disability or special requirements when booking
Scope of the Passenger Rights Protection Regulations (GACA FAQ): they apply to all flights of national air carriers and to foreign air-carrier flights that depart from a Saudi airport; inbound flights from outside Saudi Arabia are related only to baggage complaints. https://gaca.gov.sa/en/helping-and-support/new-faqs

KKIA (Riyadh) published arrival guidance (kkia.sa Guide)
KKIA states passengers should arrive 3 hours before departure for both domestic and international flights, especially with excess luggage. Check-in counters are available up to 4 hours before departure. International check-in closes 1 hour before departure; domestic check-in closes 45 minutes before departure. Follow the airline boarding pass for the terminal. KKIA has five passenger terminals. Free shuttle buses run every 10 minutes outside all terminals. Ask-Me team 24/7; unified call centre 920020090.
Source: https://www.kkia.sa/en/Guide
Other airports: follow that airport site and the airline. Do not copy KKIA's 3-hour rule onto Jeddah or Dammam unless that airport publishes it.

GACA passenger rights — numbers copied from https://gaca.gov.sa/en/passenger-rights
Baggage delay compensation (Special Drawing Rights, SDR):
- First day of delay after the reservation arrival time: equivalent of 148 SDR
- From the second day: 60 SDR per day, maximum 1288 SDR
Lost baggage: 1288 SDR. Damage or harm: not exceeding 1288 SDR. If registered baggage does not arrive within 21 days, the passenger may ask the carrier to treat it as lost. High-value items: declare them before check-in; compensation follows the declared value. The carrier is exempt if damage is from a latent/inherent defect in the baggage.

Flight cancellation compensation (in addition to refund of the ticket or remaining part, including extra fees such as seat and baggage):
- Notice from 60 days to 14 days before departure: 50% of the remaining part of the flight
- Notice from 14 days to 24 hours before departure: 75%
- Notice from 24 hours before departure until departure: 150%
If the passenger accepts an alternative flight, cancellation compensation does not apply; delay rules apply to the time difference. If the passenger rejects the alternative and terminates, the ticket is refunded in full including extras. Force majeure: carrier notifies around the clock and provides support; passenger may terminate if force majeure lasts more than 3 hours (domestic) or 6 hours (international) and get a refund without fee deduction. Technical malfunction, operational conditions, scheduling, or negligence is not force majeure.

Flight delays
- Notify at least 45 minutes before take-off with the new time; update every 30 minutes
- Delay compensation by actual arrival vs reservation: 50 SDR if 3 to 6 hours; 150 SDR if more than 6 hours
- Passenger may terminate after more than 2 hours delay and get a full refund with no fee deduction
- If delay extends over 5 hours, the passenger may treat the flight as cancelled and use cancellation compensation

Care and support (denied boarding, cancellation, or delay)
- Refreshments and drinks from the first hour
- Suitable meal if delay exceeds 3 hours from departure time
- Hotel and airport transport if delay exceeds 6 hours
If care is not provided: 10 SDR (refreshments), 30 SDR (meal), 100 SDR (hotel/transport). Money does not replace the duty to provide care.

Denied boarding / overbooking
Passenger may choose another flight of the same or another carrier (cost difference borne by the original carrier). If the passenger rejects denial of boarding and terminates: refund unused part plus 200% of that value. If the carrier provides an alternative within two hours of original departure, it is relieved from that compensation. Excluded from denial-of-boarding treatment: travel groups, unaccompanied minors, first-degree relatives plus accompanying domestic workers, special-needs passengers, persons with disability.

Downgrade on the same flight: if accepted, refund the class-price difference plus 50% of that difference. If the contract is terminated: refund unused part plus 200% of the ticket value.

Unannounced extra stopover: 100 SDR per extra stopover not announced at reservation (does not cancel delay rights). Stops for safety/emergency recorded in GACA-adopted reports are excluded.

Passengers with disabilities: mobility devices free of charge and priority; aid animals without extra fees with a competent-authority letter. If a wheelchair does not arrive on the same flight, provide an alternative immediately; 100 SDR if not provided. Denial of boarding after a confirmed ticket: 200% of total ticket value plus refund of unused part.

What this assistant must not invent
Do not invent a missed-flight fine, a terminal number, a baggage-weight allowance, or a visa fee. Ticket changes are between the passenger and the airline. Liquids and electronics rules: follow airline and airport signs and GACA notices. SDR amounts above are from GACA's published passenger-rights page; converting SDR to SAR is not on that page, so do not invent a riyal figure.

Frequently asked questions
Q: Can I get Exit Re-Entry at the airport tonight?
A: No. Issue it on Absher or Muqeem first. https://www.absher.sa https://muqeem.sa https://my.gov.sa/en/services/269423
Q: I am flying Riyadh to Jeddah only.
A: Domestic. Normally Iqama or national ID. No Exit Re-Entry. KKIA published check-in close for domestic is 45 minutes before departure; still arrive as the airline and https://www.kkia.sa/en/Guide say (KKIA: 3 hours).
Q: Which terminal in Riyadh?
A: The terminal on the boarding pass. KKIA has five terminals. Airport map: https://www.kkia.sa/en
Q: My flight was cancelled. What compensation?
A: GACA passenger rights at https://gaca.gov.sa/en/passenger-rights — refund plus 50%, 75%, or 150% of the remaining flight value depending on how late you were told. File with the airline; GACA regulations apply to national carriers and foreign carriers departing Saudi airports.
Q: Hello, I have a flight tonight.
A: Ask whether they are leaving the country or flying inside KSA. If leaving, check Absher for Exit Re-Entry or Final Exit first, then the airline app and the official airport site (kkia.sa, kaia.sa, or kfia.sa).
