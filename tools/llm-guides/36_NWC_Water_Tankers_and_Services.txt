YES, YOU CAN ORDER A WATER TANKER FROM NWC
Official page for the water tanker service: https://www.nwc.com.sa/EN/ServiceCatalog/Pages/service24.aspx
NWC runs an official water tanker service ("Submit a water tanker request"). You order it online and a licensed tanker delivers to your location.
Who can order: residential and commercial customers.
Requirements published by NWC:
1. No unpaid debt on your NWC account.
2. No continuous pumping at the site. The tanker service is for properties that do not get continuous piped supply.
How to order (app or e-branch):
1. Log in to the National Water app or the e-branch at nwc.com.sa.
2. Open "Our Services" from the top menu.
3. Tap "Water tanker".
4. Choose the tanker size.
5. Confirm the delivery location and submit the request, then pay in the app.
You can also order by calling the unified number 800 441 1110.
Price: NWC states "the tanker price depends on the size and locality". The exact price for each size is shown in the app before you pay. Do not quote a fixed price. Tell the user to read the price on the order screen.
Tanker late? File a complaint: Support and assistance, File a complaint, "Water filling stations", Tanker complaint. Also possible through 800 441 1110.
Track the order: Personal file, "Requests follow up".

Tanker without an NWC account ("Tankers account"), Makkah and Asir only
Official page for Tankers account: https://www.nwc.com.sa/EN/ServiceCatalog/Pages/service54.aspx
In the Makkah and Asir regions you can order a water tanker even if you have no water account.
Requirements: no debt and no continuous pumping.
Steps: log in, Our Services, "Tankers account", choose region and city, mark the site on the map, enter and verify the title deed number, accept the terms and submit. Also via 800 441 1110.

Septic tank full? Order a sewage tanker "Be'ati" (بيئتي) from NWC
Official page for the Be'ati sewage tanker: https://www.nwc.com.sa/EN/ServiceCatalog/pages/service50.aspx
YES, NWC DOES empty septic tanks and cesspits. Its Be'ati sewage (wastewater) tanker service is for homes with a septic tank or cesspit, or not connected to the sewage network. You do not need to hire a private truck.
How to order: log in to the National Water app or the e-branch at nwc.com.sa, open "Our Services", tap "Be'ati wastewater tanker", choose the tanker size and apply.
The tankers are licensed by NWC, tracked electronically and must unload at approved treatment plants. NWC says prices are standardised by tanker size, and the app shows the price.
NWC has launched Be'ati through its e-channels in cities including Jeddah, Madinah and Al-Baha, and is expanding it. If Be'ati does not appear for your city, call 800 441 1110.
A septic tank is not a "water incident". Use Water incidents only for sewage overflowing into the street or a network leak.

Water pumping schedule and map
- Water pumping schedule: see the daily pumping hours for your property for this month and next. Our Services, "Water pumping schedule". Also via 800 441 1110.
- Water pumping map: see pumping times for any area on the map. General services, "Water pumping map".
Use these before ordering a tanker: if your district is scheduled to pump soon, you may not need one.

No water or weak water
"Water interruption complaint": use it when water does not reach your meter from the network, or pressure is weak, during your pumping period.
Conditions: complain during the pumping time, outside announced regular or emergency maintenance, and check that the meter is running.
Steps: Support and assistance, File a complaint, "Water supply", "Water outage", submit. Also via 800 441 1110.

Leaks, sewage overflow and street problems ("Water incidents")
Report a sewage overflow, or a leak in the meter or the network: Support and assistance, "Water incidents", choose region, city and location, add photos, mark the map, send. Also via 800 441 1110.
Other reports under Support and assistance, File a complaint:
- Missing or misplaced manhole cover (sewage inspection cover).
- Rodents or bad smells from the water or sewage network.
- Meter box fault ("Meter box maintenance").
- Stolen meter. Needs a police report number first.
- Damage to your private property caused by NWC works. The owner files this.
- Remarks on the quality of NWC works (attach a photo).
- Complaint about an NWC employee or contractor.

Bills and payments
- Bills list: Our Services, "Bills list".
- High bill complaint: Support and assistance, File a complaint, "Invoicing", "High bill", then choose the bill. You must object within 60 days of the bill date and your customer data must be up to date. Also via 800 441 1110.
- Meter inspection request, if you think the meter is wrong: Our Services, "Meter inspection". Fee published by NWC: SAR 150 excluding VAT.
- Installments and partial payment of dues: Our Services, "Invoice list", Installment plan. Plans can be through NWC or partner banks. Rules: only the account owner sees it, you cannot have two installment agreements at once, missing two consecutive months cancels the agreement, and you cannot combine partial payment with an installment plan.
- Paid by mistake (refund): Support and assistance, File a complaint, Billing, "Payment by error". Needs consent of the other beneficiary.
- Account clearance (pay off everything due): Our Services, "Clear account". Owner only.
- Consumption limit: set your expected monthly use and get alerted if consumption rises above it. Our Services, "Consumption limit".
- Interactive calculator: estimate your expected monthly consumption. General services, "Interactive calculator".

New connection
- Water and wastewater connection (creates the account, connects the property and installs a meter).
  Requirements: you are the property owner, no NWC debt, stone and marble fixing completed, water outlet and wastewater outlet extended.
  Steps: Our Services, "Water and wastewater connection", region and city, map location, choose water, wastewater or both, enter and verify the deed number, attach documents, accept terms and submit.
  Fee: "determined based on the financial consideration approved by the water regulator". It is shown when the request reaches payment. Do not guess it.
- Water-only and wastewater-only connection requests are also available. A water-only request also needs the building permit attached.
- Installments for the household connection fee: NWC published a company plan (minimum SAR 4,100 to qualify, down payment from SAR 3,500, installments from SAR 200, no NWC interest) and bank-card plans with partner banks. Bank plans may include bank fees. Choose on the connection-tracking page after the bill SMS.
- Register an unregistered connection: if a pipe or meter at your property is not linked to a customer record. Our Services, "Registering unregistered connection", choose your relation (owner, beneficiary or reporter), service type, region, city, map, optional account and meter numbers, attach a photo and documents. NWC has warned that not registering within 30 days of the notice on the meter can lead to disconnection.

Account, tenants and moving house
- Add an existing account as owner: Our Services, "Add existing account", enter the account number or serial number or pick the location on the map, choose "Property owner", enter the deed or property registration number, upload documents.
- Add an existing account as a beneficiary (for example a tenant): Our Services, add existing account, choose beneficiary "with or without financial liability". The current owner must approve.
- Beneficiary management (owner adds a tenant or family member): account details, "Edit Beneficiary Management", "Add a Beneficiary", enter the ID number and choose with or without financial liability. The beneficiary must approve.
- Not my account: if an account in your name is not yours, Our Services, "This account doesn't belong to me", pick it and submit.
- Property ownership transfer (new owner): Our Services, "Property Transfer", fill in the form and attach documents.
- Rental contracts on Ejar: a documented Ejar lease can include the electricity and water meters so the tenant becomes the beneficiary. If the tenant is not linked after moving in, use "Add existing account as a beneficiary" above.

Meter services
- Temporary meter closure (for example an empty house): owner only, no debt. Our Services, "Temporary meter closure", give the reason.
- Reopen a temporarily closed meter: Our Services, temporary meter closure, request re-opening. Fee published by NWC: SAR 100 excluding VAT.
- Remove the meter for renovation: owner, no debt, attach the restoration permit. The actual cost of removal and re-installation is set by NWC.
- Service separation (termination, for demolition): attach the demolition permit, no debt. Through customer service centres.
- Change the connection diameter, or move the connection within the property: owner, no debt. Fees follow the regulator-approved schedule.
- Edit property information: billing units, building units, land area, building area, and the property classification (personal, commercial, government). Owner or authorised person, with deed, commercial registration or building permit as relevant.

Business and other
- Commercial enterprise registration: Business accounts, add the commercial registration number and documents.
- Appointment with NWC leaders: Our Services, "Appointment with company leaders".
- Giga-project requests are handled separately on nwc.com.sa.

Frequently asked questions
Q: Can I order a water tanker online in Saudi Arabia?
A: Yes. Use the National Water app or nwc.com.sa: Our Services, Water tanker, choose the size, submit and pay. You can also call 800 441 1110. You need no unpaid NWC bills, and the site must not have continuous pumping.
Q: How much is a water tanker?
A: NWC says the price depends on the tanker size and your area. The app shows the price for each size before you pay.
Q: My tanker has not arrived.
A: Check "Requests follow up" in the app, then file a tanker delay complaint (Support and assistance, File a complaint, Water filling stations, Tanker complaint) or call 800 441 1110.
Q: I have no NWC account. Can I still get a tanker?
A: In the Makkah and Asir regions, yes, through the "Tankers account" service using your title deed number. Elsewhere, add or register the property account first.
Q: My septic tank is full. What do I do?
A: Order NWC's Be'ati sewage tanker in the National Water app: Our Services, Be'ati wastewater tanker, choose the size and apply. NWC provides this service itself. If it is not listed for your city, call 800 441 1110.
Q: Water is not coming today.
A: Check the pumping schedule in the app first. If it is your pumping time, there is no announced maintenance and the meter is not running, file a water interruption complaint.
Q: There is a leak or sewage overflow in the street.
A: Report it in the app under Support and assistance, Water incidents, with a photo and the map location, or call 800 441 1110.
