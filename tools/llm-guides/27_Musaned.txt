What Musaned is
Musaned is the official platform for recruiting and managing domestic workers (housemaids, house drivers, cooks, nannies, and similar household jobs listed by the Minister). Saudi citizens and eligible residents who employ domestic workers use Musaned. Private-sector company workers on Qiwa contracts are a different track. Do not use Musaned Article 22 (one month per four years) to answer a normal company-job end-of-service question. Company EOSB is Labour Law Articles 84–85 on laws.boe.gov.sa.

Costs
Musaned recruitment pages state service cost “Varies depending on occupation and nationality”. Do not invent a recruitment package price. The office and Musaned show the current cost. Employer must have an issued visa and an active Musaned account for several hire services.

Domestic Workers Regulation — numbers copied from Musaned Labor Education
https://www.musaned.com.sa/en/labor-education
- Under 21: prohibited to employ (Article 6)
- Contract is fixed-term; if duration missing, one year from start, then renewable (Article 7)
- Arabic text is the official contract reference (Article 7)
- Probation: up to 90 days in writing; either party may terminate; not more than once with the same employer unless duties change (Article 9)
- Hours: not more than 10 hours in a single day; no more than 5 consecutive hours without a break of at least 30 minutes; total rest not less than 8 continuous hours; breaks are not working hours (Article 10)
- Weekly rest: one full paid day, at least 24 continuous hours; if the worker works that day, substitute rest or cash by agreement (Article 11)
- Leave: after two years, if both want to renew, 30 days leave or cash at the end of the relationship; round-trip ticket home every two years for that leave (Article 12)
- Sick leave: up to 30 days (continuous or not) per medical report: first 15 days full pay, remaining days half pay; employer may end the contract if illness exceeds 30 days and must pay the return ticket and settle dues (Article 13)
- Employer pays recruitment, profession-change, transfer, residency, work-permit fees and renewals, and fines caused by the employer (Article 15)
- Employer must not keep the passport or personal documents (Article 15)
- Suitable accommodation and food or a cash food allowance (Article 15)
- Wage monthly in Saudi riyals by methods the Ministry determines (Articles 17–18)
- Deductions only in listed cases and not more than one quarter of the wage unless a judgment says otherwise (Article 20)
- EOSB: one month's last salary for every four consecutive years with the employer, paid when the relationship ends (Article 22)
- Settlement: one week if the contract ended by the employer; two weeks if the worker ended it (Article 23)
- Unlawful termination compensation if the contract does not set an amount: two months' salary (Article 25)
- Employer violation penalties: fine not exceeding SAR 20,000 and/or recruitment ban up to three years, or a permanent ban; may double on repeat; fines multiply by number of affected persons (Article 29)
- Worker violation: fine not exceeding SAR 2,000 and/or ban on working in the Kingdom (Article 30)
- Claims: generally not accepted more than 12 months after the contract ends unless an acceptable excuse or the defendant admits (Article 5)
- Month = 30 days; year = 12 months; Gregorian calendar unless the contract says otherwise (Articles 1–2)

Complaints
Domestic-worker disputes go through Musaned / HRSD domestic channels, not ordinary Qiwa private-sector amicable settlement (HRSD friendly-settlement page excludes domestic labour).

Frequently asked questions
Q: Is Musaned the same as Qiwa transfer?
A: No. Musaned is for domestic workers. Company employee transfers go through Qiwa https://qiwa.sa
Q: What is domestic EOSB?
A: Musaned Labor Education Article 22: one month's last salary for every four consecutive years.
Q: Hello, I want a housemaid.
A: Use https://www.musaned.com.sa and follow licensed recruitment options. Cost varies by occupation and nationality on the service page.
