What Qiwa does
Qiwa is the MHRSD portal for private-sector labour services: authenticated contracts, work permits, employee transfer, Nitaqat (Saudization) certificates, occupation correction, skill verification. HRSD Contract Management: establishment creates the contract (job title, duration, hours, financial obligations, extra items); employee approves, rejects, or requests amendment on Qiwa Afrad. If both agree, the contract is certified by HRSD. Establishment must be running; employee must not already have a valid Qiwa contract.

Employee transfer — HRSD published steps
HRSD Employee Transfer: fully automated, no paperwork listed on that service page.
HRSD “Transfer of services from another employer (Freedom of movement)”:
- New employer submits a job offer on Qiwa
- Worker logs in and accepts or rejects
- Existing employer is notified
- System verifies requirements; after approval, data is updated and the new work permit is issued
Service fees on that HRSD page: Free. Duration: Immediately. Always read the live Qiwa request for whose turn it is.

Practical status lines workers see (KSA Guide notes / Qiwa screens — confirm on the user's request, do not treat as BOE law)
Pending laborer approval → worker must accept (offers can expire; read the deadline on screen).
Pending current employer's approval → when consent is required.
Pending notice period completion → a contractual/legal notice may still be running.
Pending completing transfer in Absher by new employer → new employer finishes sponsorship change in Absher/Muqeem and pays any transfer fee shown there.
The new employer needs an active CR, GOSI registration, and wage-protection compliance as Qiwa requires. Transfer fee is paid by the new employer at the amount shown in Qiwa or Absher. Do not invent SAR 2000 or any other figure.

Labour Reform Initiative — official ministerial decree (HRSD English PDF)
https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2023-02/E51848.pdf
Ministerial Decree (Minister’s Office No. 51484, dated 19/03/1442H / 05/11/2020G), in force 01/08/1442H corresponding to 14 March 2021G, amending Implementing Regulations Article 14 (Second):
1. An expatriate worker may work for another employer without a required waiting period if the current employer agrees, unless Nitaqat controls say otherwise.
2. Upon expiration of the authenticated employment contract, the expatriate may work for another employer without the current employer's consent.
3. Without current employer consent, considering Labour Law Article 77, if: (a) employed for twelve months from the date of entry into the Kingdom; and (b) notifies the current employer at least ninety days before terminating the contractual relation, unless both parties agree otherwise.
HRSD news on LRI also states the initiative regulates job mobility and Exit/Re-Entry, and that services are through Absher and Qiwa. https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/770957

Unpaid wages and other no-consent cases
Qiwa's live request lists the cases the system can auto-check. Official pieces you can cite:
- Expired authenticated contract: no current-employer consent (decree above)
- 12 months from entry + 90 days' notice: no consent, subject to Article 77 (decree above)
- Wage Protection / Mudad is HRSD's approved reference for wage disputes; documented wage clause may be enforced on Najiz after 30 days (full non-payment) or 90 days (partial) per HRSD's 2025 enforceable-contract guideline
If the user's Qiwa screen names a specific no-consent case (for example unpaid wages or expired Iqama/work permit), follow that screen. Do not invent extra cases that are not on the decree, HRSD page, or the user's Qiwa request.

After Qiwa accepts, the new employer still completes Absher/Muqeem so the Iqama sponsor changes.

Frequently asked questions
Q: I accepted the contract. Why is it not finished?
A: Worker acceptance is one step. HRSD: existing employer is notified; the system verifies; then the new work permit is issued. Absher/Muqeem may still be pending. Read the Qiwa status line.
Q: Can my current employer block me forever?
A: They can object when the law/Qiwa gives them that role. They cannot block a transfer Qiwa accepts as a no-consent case (expired authenticated contract; or 12 months from entry plus 90 days' notice per the 2021 decree, subject to Article 77).
Q: Where do I accept?
A: https://qiwa.sa individual account, Employee Transfer, view the contract, accept or reject.
Q: Hello, I want to change sponsor.
A: Greet them, then ask for the Qiwa status line.
