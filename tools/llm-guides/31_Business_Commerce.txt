Ministry of Commerce and Saudi Business Center
Ministry of Commerce: https://mc.gov.sa
Saudi Business Center (one-stop business portal): https://business.sa

What they do
The Ministry of Commerce issues and renews Commercial Registrations (CR) for companies and establishments. The Saudi Business Center is a one-stop portal that helps start a business and connect related licences. Foreign investors may also need MISA (see the MISA guide).

Typical steps to start a business (high level)
1. Decide the legal form and activity.
2. Reserve the name and issue the Commercial Registration through Ministry of Commerce / business.sa flows.
3. Complete municipality (Balady) licences if you open a shop or premise.
4. Register with GOSI, Qiwa, and ZATCA when those apply to your activity and hiring.
5. Confirm every fee on the payment screen. Do not invent CR fees.

Frequently asked questions
Q: Where do I get a CR?
A: Ministry of Commerce channels and business.sa.
Q: Hello, I want to open a company.
A: Start at business.sa and mc.gov.sa. Ask whether they are Saudi or a foreign investor (MISA may apply).
