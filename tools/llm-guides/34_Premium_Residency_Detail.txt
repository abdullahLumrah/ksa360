Premium Residency — products and official portal
Official platform: Premium Residency Center https://pr.gov.sa
National Platform listings also describe requesting Premium Residency and unlimited-duration issuing.

What it is
Premium Residency lets eligible foreigners live in Saudi Arabia without a traditional employer sponsor (kafeel). It is not citizenship. Restricted Saudi-only occupations still apply.

Products listed on pr.gov.sa
Exceptional Competence (Special Talent), Gifted / Talent, Business Investor, Entrepreneur, Real Estate Owner, Unlimited Duration, and Limited Duration (renewable). Choose the product on the portal; each has its own evidence checklist.

Fees commonly published on government service pages
Unlimited Duration issuing is listed on the National Platform with a cost of 800000 (SAR one-time on that service page). Limited Duration is widely published as an annual fee — confirm the live amount on pr.gov.sa before paying. Category products such as talent, investor, entrepreneur, and real-estate owner show their own application fees on the portal. Do not invent salary or investment thresholds. Read the product page.

General eligibility themes
Valid passport, usually age 21+ for some products, clean record, financial solvency where required, medical insurance / medical report as requested, and legal status if applying from inside the Kingdom. Product-specific proof (salary, investment, property deed, awards) is mandatory.

Application steps
1. Create an account on pr.gov.sa and pick a product.
2. Upload documents and pay the application fee shown.
3. Wait for review. Some National Platform pages cite about 90 working days for processing windows — treat that as a published estimate, not a promise.
4. After approval, pay the residency fee and complete biometrics / card steps as instructed.

Salary question — answer this clearly
Q: My salary is SAR 40,000 a month. Am I eligible for Premium Residency?
A: Direct answer first: SAR 40,000 a month is above the Special Talent (Exceptional Competence) wage floor for health and scientific specialties. That floor in the Premium Residency Center implementing regulations is a total monthly wage of not less than SAR 35,000. Salary alone does not grant the product. You still need an employment contract, a bachelor's degree or higher, and the Center's minimum points. SAR 40,000 does not meet the executive track (total annual wage not less than SAR 960,000). Unlimited-duration and one-year products are paid products, not salary products.

Special Talent wage floors (Premium Residency Center implementing regulations)
- Health and scientific specialties: employment contract + monthly wage not less than SAR 35,000 + bachelor's or higher + minimum points.
- Researchers: employment contract + monthly wage not less than SAR 14,000 + bachelor's or higher + minimum points.
- Executives: employment contract + total annual wage not less than SAR 960,000.

Product fees in the same regulations (confirm live on pr.gov.sa)
- Permanent / unlimited duration: SAR 800,000
- Renewable one-year: SAR 100,000
- Special talent, gifted, investor, entrepreneur, real estate: SAR 4,000 application/product fee in that table

Investor / property floors in the same regulations (not salary)
- Investor: invest at least SAR 7,000,000 inside the Kingdom as determined by the Center.
- Real estate: ownership or usufruct not less than SAR 4,000,000.

How to answer a salary question
1. Say yes/no against the matching wage floor in one sentence.
2. Name the product (Special Talent vs paid unlimited/one-year vs investor vs property).
3. List the other required conditions.
4. Tell the user to confirm the live checklist on https://pr.gov.sa

Frequently asked questions
Q: Do I need a sponsor?
A: No employer sponsor for Premium Residency itself.
Q: Does it give citizenship?
A: No.
Q: Hello, Premium Residency cost?
A: Open pr.gov.sa, pick the product, and use the fee on that page. Unlimited duration is listed at SAR 800,000 one-time. The implementing regulations also list SAR 100,000 for the renewable one-year product and SAR 4,000 for Special Talent and several category products. Confirm on pr.gov.sa before paying.
