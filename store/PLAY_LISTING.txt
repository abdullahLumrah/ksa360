FULL DESCRIPTION
============================================================
KSA 360 is the all-in-one companion for expats living in Saudi Arabia.

Buy and sell nearby in Marketplace — cars, furniture, phones, and more. Join community pages, follow jobs, find food near you, and keep prayer times for your city on your profile.

Built for daily life in the Kingdom.

Marketplace
Browse and post listings for cars, electronics, home items, and real estate. Chat with sellers after you sign in. Meet in public and inspect before you pay.

Community
Follow pages run by people who already live here. Like, reply, and share posts about housing, visas, schools, and weekends.

Work and daily needs
Scan jobs across Saudi cities. Find clinics and international schools near you. Open Daily in KSA for apps, shops, and offers.

Food and play
Eat nearby — Arab, Chinese, Desi, and more. Discover dunes, karting, snow, cinema, and malls around your city.

Prayer and language
Umm Al-Qura prayer times for your location. Switch the whole app to Arabic — text and right-to-left — from Profile. English stays the default.

KSA 360 is made by Lumrah for expats who want one place for the stuff that used to live in ten WhatsApp groups.
